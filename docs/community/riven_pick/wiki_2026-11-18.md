## Extra Hit (https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Extra_Hit)
- "Extra Hit is a unique buff that adds an additional hit to the target, dealing a percentage of the original damage value and independently rolling Status Effects."
- "Extra Hit Damage = Weapon Hit Damage × Extra Hit Percentage × (1 + Faction Damage Bonuses)"
- "Weapon Hit Damage = Base Damage × [1 + Elemental Bonuses + Unmodded Impact Distribution × Impact Bonuses + Unmodded Puncture Distribution × Puncture Bonuses + Unmodded Slash Distribution × Slash Bonuses] × (1 + Damage Bonuses) × (1 + Faction Damage Bonuses) × Additional Multipliers"
- "Additional Multipliers include modded critical multiplier on Critical Hit, multipliers on Enemy Body Parts, and external weapon buffs that do not fit into other bonus categories."
- "Faction Damage Bonuses appear twice in the equation, affecting the final damage twice."
- "Damage over Time status effects created by an Extra Hit will use the Extra Hit Damage as Modded Base Damage in their damage calculations. This allows Elemental Bonuses to contribute to the Modded Base Damage of a status effect when they normally would not. The Damage over Time formula applies Faction Damage Bonuses again, allowing status effects created by an Extra Hit to "triple-dip" on these bonuses."
- "Damage over Time status effects created by an Extra Hit will use the matching Elemental Bonuses of the weapon that triggered the hit in their damage calculations. For example, with Toxic Lash, a weapon dealing 100 base damage with Infected Clip will deal an Extra Hit of 100 × (1 + .9) × 0.3 = 57 damage. The resulting Toxin status effect will deal 57 × (1 + .9) × 0.5 = 54.15 damage per tick, as the weapon has a 90% Toxin Damage Bonus. This also works if the mod is part of a combined element, such as having both an Infected Clip and Cryo Rounds, as the weapon has both a 90% Toxin Damage Bonus and a 90% Cold Damage Bonus that combine. However, an already combined Elemental Bonus such as Nourish does not count as a matching Elemental Bonus for a Toxin status effect, though it will still contribute to the Extra Hit's damage."
- "Since damage from Extra Hits is dealt as a separate damage instance, it does not combine with elements on the weapon, nor shift the weapon's damage distribution for the purpose of status chance."
- "Each hit of Multishot triggers a separate Extra Hit."
- "If a hit that would trigger an Extra Hit kills the enemy, the Extra Hit will not be triggered. This includes any status effects the Extra Hit would have created."
- "Status Damage modded on the weapon affects the damage of status effects created by Extra Hit effects."
- Table row: "Resupply | 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% as selectable Elemental Damage, doubled for Sniper Rifles | Selected element's status effect (guaranteed)"

## Resupply (https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resupply)
- "Throw two Elemental Ammo Packs that instantly refill the active weapon's magazine, while granting the weapon an additional instance of the selected Elemental Damage and Status Effect. Reload clears the effect."
- "Cyte-09 expends 50 energy to instantly reload his currently equipped weapon while throwing forward 2 ammo packs."
- "Reloading the weapon (default R) while buffed by Resupply will clear the elemental damage bonus."
- "Resupply ammo packs only refill the current magazine, and will not restore any reserve ammo."
- "Casting Resupply is a One-Handed Action. As such, it can be used while performing various maneuvers and actions without interruption."
- "Hold the ability to open a Gear wheel to select the elemental damage type of the reload and ammo packs: Heat, Cold, Electricity, Toxin, Blast, Radiation, Gas, Magnetic, Viral, Corrosive, randomized, or previously selected. Tap the ability to deploy the last selected element." / "By default, tap cast starts as randomize."
- "Resupply's forced Status Effect will not apply if the enemy is killed in a single bullet"
- "Resupply's forced Status Effect is calculated using the extra instance's damage value as base. Main and extra hit have their own independent status procs. For example modded Heat with forced Electricity won't create Radiation. Faction elemental weaknesses affect the extra instance's base damage. A Murmur enemy with electric weakness will take : Base damage of extra instance x 0.5 x 1.5 x other DoT multipliers for the DoT damage calculation."
- "Resupply has a 0.35 second cooldown between casts."

## Accuracy (https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Accuracy)
- "In WARFRAME, spread is internally represented as an angle in degrees from the reticle, with each weapon having a defined minimum (first-shot) and maximum spread. Minimum spread is represented by the Deviation With Aim stat while maximum spread is represented by the Max Deviation stat."
- "Note that both of these stats both refer to deviation while aiming; weapons typically have much higher deviation when fired without aiming."
- "In addition, the faster a weapon fires, the larger the size of the "cone" around the reticle."
- "Shotguns, as well as any hitscan-based weapons with multishot mods, fire at least one of their pellets close to if not directly centered on the reticle, with all other pellets taking random trajectories within the spread cone."
- In-game description quoted there: "Consecutive shots reduce accuracy. Improve accuracy while using Aim."
